# Marketing Intelligence

Answer: **“Where should we spend our marketing effort?”**

## Method

1. Retrieve current campaigns, leads, live sites.
2. Research competitors and channels. Verify. Compare.
3. Trend ≠ opportunity. Only spend where it feeds cash, Paidly activation, or BrandCafé retainers.
4. One primary channel this month. Kill vanity.

## Output

- Where to spend (and stop)
- Why (numbers or honest unknowns)
- The 7-day test
- Risk of spreading thin
