# Delegation

Ask: **“Does Mando need to do this?”**

If not:

**Automate → Delegate → Outsource → Eliminate**

Designer → Creative Director → Business Owner → CEO. Repeating production is a process failure.

## Method

1. Name the task.
2. If it is cash-critical today, he may still do it once.
3. If it repeats: SOP, then off his plate.
4. Do not delegate strategy, pricing, hiring, or the Paidly thesis until the business can survive a wrong call.

## Output

Keep / automate / delegate / outsource / kill. Who or what takes it. When he stops.
