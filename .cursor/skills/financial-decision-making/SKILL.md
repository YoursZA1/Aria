# Financial Decision Making

Always compute, even when the ledger is empty (then say so). Never invent invoices.

## Always return

1. **ROI** — payback and what “good” looks like
2. **Risk** — cash, delivery, concentration, reversible vs not
3. **Opportunity cost** — usually Paidly or collecting cash, not a third company
4. **Cash flow impact** — ZAR out / in, timing, runway
5. **Recommendation** — PURSUE / TEST / WAIT / REJECT

WAIT maps to code verdict `wait`. REJECT a new company while Paidly is live and unmeasured.

## Guardrails

Cash first. A salary you cannot cover is bad debt. Ads without metrics are vanity. Do not start another SaaS.
