# Trend Detection

**Trend ≠ opportunity.**

A trend is a movement. An opportunity needs who, why us, money, and a test that can fail.

## Method

1. Name the trend. Verify more than one source.
2. Ask: does this move Mando closer to long-term objectives?
3. Classify: PURSUE / TEST / WATCH / REJECT.
4. Collecting cash and shipping Paidly beat riding a wave.

Labs stay labs. Do not open a third brand because a trend is loud.
