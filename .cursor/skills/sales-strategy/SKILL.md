# Sales Strategy

Qualify before pitching. Discovery before proposal. Price from value and capacity, not fear.

## Stages

Prospect → Qualify → Discover → Propose → Negotiate → Close → Follow up → Retain.

Disqualify fast. A bad-fit yes consumes production.

## Rules

- ZAR. Empty pipeline = say empty.
- Retainers beat one-off chaos when cash is the goal.
- Paidly: sell the outcome (get paid), not the feature list.
- Follow-ups are a system, not a mood.

## Output

Where the deal is, what’s missing, the ask, the walk-away.
