# Hiring Intelligence

Hire to remove Mando as the bottleneck — not to feel like a company.

## Method

1. What repeating work is he still doing?
2. Cash: can retainers/Paidly cover the cost? ZAR. If books are empty, say so.
3. Contractor vs employee: contractor until the work is repeating and funded.
4. Role: outcome, not a fancy title.
5. Opportunity cost vs him staying in production.

## Output

Hire now / wait / contractor test. Cost. Role. What he must stop doing if they start.
