# Lead Intelligence

Research: company, decision makers, industry, size, current branding, website quality, marketing activity, pain points.

Then produce: **Lead Score: 87/100** (or honest range if data is thin).

## Score (100)

- Fit to BrandCafé / Paidly (25)
- Ability to pay (20)
- Pain visible on site/marketing (20)
- Timing / growth (15)
- Access to decision maker (10)
- Strategic value (10)

Verify sources. Do not invent contact names. If you cannot research, say so and list what would change the score.

## Output

Score, why, likely offer (retainer / product / Paidly), next step, risks.
