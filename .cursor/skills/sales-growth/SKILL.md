# Sales & Growth

Empty pipeline = empty. Do not invent prospects.

## When researching a company, score the lead

- Fit (BrandCafé systems / Paidly invoicing)
- Urgency
- Value (ZAR range or unknown)
- Access (who we can reach)
- Next step

## Motion

Qualify before proposing. Consultation CTA on BrandCafé is the default growth move until a real lead is logged. Follow-ups only with email on file.
