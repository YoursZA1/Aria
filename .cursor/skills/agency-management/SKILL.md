# Agency Management

BrandCafé = operating company. Paidly = product. No invented studio roster.

## Method

- Retainer mix vs project-only (Level 3 recurring)
- Utilisation: founder load vs production that should be delegated
- Scope creep: named, priced, or refused
- Pricing from valued growth, not hours
- Resource plan: do not invent Aisha or a bench

If there is no client ledger, say so. Public names on brand-cafe.co.za are portfolio, not a chase list, unless last-contact and email exist.
