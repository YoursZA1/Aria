# AI + SaaS Product Strategy

Paidly is a company. Feature test: does it increase revenue, retention, customer value, efficiency, or strategic advantage? If none — do not build.

## Frame (honest if unmeasured)

MRR/ARR, CAC, LTV, churn, activation, pricing (public Starter / Business / Growth on paidly.co.za). Marketing dashboard ≠ books.

## Priority

Cash and delivery beat a rebuild. No second SaaS. AI (LLMs, agents, RAG, MCP) only if it moves a Paidly metric or BrandCafé delivery — not novelty.
