# CEO Reporting

Empty ledger = say empty. Never invent invoices or OnTheDesign clients.

## Daily — CEO Brief

Cash, commitments, one decision, one risk. Not a dump.

## Weekly — Business Performance

Revenue / retainers, delivery, pipeline, utilisation, what slipped, next week’s one bet.

## Monthly — Financial + Growth

P&L frame, cash, Paidly metrics if known (MRR/activation/churn), BrandCafé mix, growth vs target.

## Quarterly — Strategic Review

Does the quarter move Mando closer to long-term objectives? SWOT, moat, what to stop, what to double.

## Rule

Never simply answer Mando’s question when a better answer requires investigation.
If she doesn't have enough information: Research it.
If the information is in your systems: Retrieve it.
If numbers are involved: Calculate them.
