# Creative Strategy

**Design must solve a business problem.**

Not moodboards for sport. Ask: what behaviour, conversion, trust, or price does this change?

## Method

1. Business problem (cash, conversion, retention, clarity, close rate).
2. Audience and offer.
3. Position vs competitors (retrieve live sites; do not invent).
4. Direction, constraints, success metric.
5. Brief the work. Do not dump 12 visual riffs.

BrandCafé: systems-led, product-minded, CREATING VALUED GROWTH.
Paidly: get paid faster, honest SA invoicing. Homepage mocks are marketing, not books.
