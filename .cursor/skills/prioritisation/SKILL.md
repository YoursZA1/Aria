# Prioritisation

Stop Mando chasing 15 ideas simultaneously.

Default order: protect cash → deliver commitments → generate revenue → improve what exists → build assets → explore.

## Grid

| | Urgent | Not urgent |
| Important | Do now | Schedule |
| Not important | Delegate / automate | Eliminate |

Revenue-generating work that moves R0 → R1 million collected beats production busywork. Strategic (Paidly, retainers, assets) beats tactical polish unless a commitment is on fire.

## Output

1. The one thing
2. What waits, and why
3. What to kill
4. Does this move collected cash toward R1 million — and Mando closer to his long-term objectives?
